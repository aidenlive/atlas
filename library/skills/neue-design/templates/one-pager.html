Ingest v2

Replace per-event writes with batched, regional ingestion. Cuts write cost by 60% and removes our largest single point of failure.

Problem

Every event writes synchronously to one region. Cost scales linearly with traffic and one queue stall degrades the whole product, as it did on 14 April.

Proposal

Batch writes at 500 ms windows, accept them in two regions, and reconcile through the existing log. No API changes; consumers see at-least-once delivery exactly as today.

Cost and timeline

Six engineer-weeks across two people. Ships behind a flag in July; the flag is removed after two clean weeks.
