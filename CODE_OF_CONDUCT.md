# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be the colleague you would want reviewing your own pull request.

## What we expect

- Critique the work, never the person. "This root has a status file (`PJ-03`)"
  is feedback; "you never read the standard" is not.
- Assume competence. Everyone here is capable and working with incomplete
  information, including you.
- Disagree with reasons, and cite the rule. A rule you dislike can be changed by
  changing the file.
- Give the next person a route. If you block something, say what would unblock
  it.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or sustained disruption of
discussion.

## Reporting

Raise a concern with any principal named in [`admin.yaml`](admin.yaml). Reports
are handled discreetly, and the person reporting is told what happened next.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in review, and anywhere someone is representing
this work.
